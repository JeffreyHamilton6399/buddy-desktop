An AI that lives on your desktop, not on someone's server.

Buddy is a small orb that sits above your other windows. Click it, or say “Hey Buddy”, and it answers out loud. There is no API key, no account and no sign-in — it brings its own model, its own voice and its own ears, and keeps working with the network unplugged.

Download macOS, Windows and Linux. Free, MIT licensed.

What it does

Answers without asking anyone
Llama 3.2 runs inside the app through llama.cpp. Three larger models are a click away if your machine can take them, and an Ollama you already run shows up alongside them.
Speaks in a real voice
Not the robotic one your operating system ships with. Kokoro generates the speech on your own CPU, with 28 voices to choose between.
Answers to its name
Whisper listens for “Hey Buddy” on-device. Ask in the same breath — “Hey Buddy, what's the capital of Peru” — and it skips the pleasantries and just answers.
Keeps your conversations
Saved to your disk as readable JSON. Browse them, reopen them, delete them. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

Where each part runs

Buddy has three jobs, and out of the box all three happen on your computer. You can move any of them to a cloud API. Nothing asks you to.

Job Default If you go looking
Thinking Llama 3.2, in the app Ollama, a cloud API
Speaking Kokoro, in the app your OS's voices, a cloud API
Listening Whisper, in the app your own Whisper server, a cloud API, off
Your chats your disk never uploaded

The exception is the first launch, which downloads the model, the voice and the ears from HuggingFace — about a gigabyte in total, once. After that you can unplug the network and Buddy carries on. Please do check. There is no telemetry and no account.

Point a job at a cloud API and that job stops being private: those messages go to the provider to be processed. What stays true either way is that there is no Buddy server and no proxy — nothing of yours passes through us, because there is no “us” in the path. The same goes for the wake word: by default the audio is transcribed in the app, and setting listening to off means the microphone is never opened at all.
